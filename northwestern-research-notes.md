# Northwestern Essay Research Notes (2026–27)

## Requirements
- **The Common App personal statement is OPTIONAL for Northwestern** (since 2023). Northwestern reviews it if submitted.
- **Required:** one supplement, ≤300 words:
  > "What aspects of your background (your identity, your school setting, your community, your household, etc.) have most shaped how you see yourself engaging in Northwestern's community, be it academically, extracurricularly, culturally, politically, socially, or otherwise?"
- **Optional (recommended to answer 2), ≤200 words each:**
  1. What would you paint on The Rock, and why?
  2. Interdisciplinary: who might be some ideal classmates or collaborators?
  3. Communities and belonging you'd connect with on campus
  4. What about Northwestern's location (Lake Michigan, Evanston, Chicago) is compelling?
  5. How might your background contribute to diverse perspectives?

## What Northwestern reads for
- **Connecting the dots** (Dean Liz Kinsley): every essay should add something the others don't, linking past, present and future.
- **Extreme specificity:** "the more specific you are, the more specific you are about what makes you special."
- **Personality:** "if you're funny, be funny; if you're intense, be intense."
- **Anecdotes** that put the reader in your shoes.
- **Ordinary moments, extraordinary insight.** A memorable admitted essay was about being bad at a frozen yogurt job.
- **Research Northwestern:** name specific courses, professors, programs and student groups, and show how you'd actually show up.

## Strategy for Crystal
| Essay | Story | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Common App personal statement (submit to NU; required elsewhere) | **Tattoo / "Honesty Is Faith"** | Distinctive, philosophy fit, shows who she is now |
| **NU required 300-word "background" essay** | **Korean American identity: Queens → Roslyn, Walmart "Keep walking," Superfood** | The prompt asks for exactly this, and it adds a "dot" the tattoo essay doesn't |
| NU optional #5 (diverse perspectives) or #3 (communities) | Superfood / translating / HAVEN / Korean community | Concrete campus connections |
| NU optional #2 (interdisciplinary) | Anthropology + philosophy | Name specific Weinberg courses and professors |
